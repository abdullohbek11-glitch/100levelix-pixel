1. What this app is

Levelix HR is an attendance app for employees of companies that use the Levelix HR system. There is no sign-up inside the app: your employer's HR administrator creates your account and gives you a login and password. The app works with your employer's HR system, and your data is stored there and in a connected face verification service.

3. Face data

A face is biometric data, so we describe it separately.

  • When you register your face and on every check in or check out, the app captures a few frames from the front camera and sends them to the server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The server detects the face, checks that a live person is present, and compares it with your registered face.
  • Camera frames are not stored. They are processed in real time and discarded.
  • A numerical face template is stored. This is a set of numbers computed for comparison; it is not a photo. It is used only inside the face verification service. The HR system stores only a reference to it.
  • The app does not use your phone's Face ID or fingerprint. Face verification is done entirely on our server.

4. Who receives your data

Your data is not sold and is not shared with third parties for advertising or analytics. Your employer uses it as an employer (working time records and HR processes). Hosting provider for the HR system: Odoo SA (Odoo.sh). The face verification service is hosted by UzCloud (Uzbekistan).

6. Your rights and account deletion

You may ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. Contact your company's HR department or 100levelix@gmail.com.

The app does not create or delete accounts itself. The HR administrator creates your account and deletes it when you leave the company or on your request. On request, your face template is also removed from the face verification service.

7. Children

The app is intended for working employees and is not directed at children.
