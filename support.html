Common questions

I do not have a login

Your account is created by your company's HR administrator. Ask them for a login and password.

The app says my phone or face is waiting for approval

A new phone and a new face registration are reviewed by an administrator before attendance works. Ask your HR administrator to approve them.

The app says I am outside the work zone

Move closer to your assigned work zone, make sure Location Services are on and set to precise location, and try again.

I forgot my password

Ask your HR administrator to set a new password for you.
